About

Bridging research and hands-on engineering — from autonomous driving, to warehouse robotics, to AR devices, and now autonomous robots on construction sites.

Currently a Postdoc at ETH Zurich (RSL), I specialize in the navigation and localization of mobile robots on construction sites. My PhD from the University of Bonn focused on visual place recognition within complex, dynamic environments.

Construction sites are characterized by repetitive, featureless geometries. This project develops robust localization frameworks for robots and AR devices to navigate visually degraded, highly ambiguous environments.
